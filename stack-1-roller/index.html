Why Roller Blinds Remain the Most Popular Choice

Roller blinds account for the largest share of residential window coverings in South Africa. They suit almost every room: blockout rollers for bedrooms, sheer rollers for living areas, and moisture-resistant fabrics for kitchens and bathrooms. The mechanism is simple, durable, and easy to maintain.

What to Look for When Ordering Online

Accurate measuring is the single most important factor. A good online blind retailer will provide clear measuring guides and back it up with a guarantee. Custom Blinds offers a Custom Fit Guarantee: if your blinds arrive too large, they trim them free of charge; if too small, you receive 5% off the replacement.

Beyond fit, consider fabric weight, light filtration level, and whether you want a chain-operated or motorised mechanism. Motorised roller blinds work with home automation systems and are increasingly popular in open-plan homes where reaching high windows is impractical.

Blockout vs Sheer: Choosing the Right Fabric

Blockout roller blinds use an opaque fabric that eliminates external light entirely. They are the standard recommendation for bedrooms, nurseries, and home cinemas. Sheer rollers, by contrast, filter light while maintaining outward visibility during the day, making them ideal for living rooms and studies.

Many homeowners opt for a combination: blockout in private rooms and sheer in communal spaces. Some choose a duo roller system, which pairs both fabrics on a single headrail for rooms that need flexibility throughout the day.
