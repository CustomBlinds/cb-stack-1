Blockout Roller Blinds in South Africa: How to Get a Properly Dark Room

Published 11 August 2026

Blockout is a property of the fabric, not the colour. A white blockout roller blind will darken a room just as well as a charcoal one, because the light is stopped by an opaque coating laminated into the cloth rather than by the shade you see. That single fact clears up most of the confusion people have when they shop for a dark bedroom, and it is the right place to start.

Where blockout earns its keep

The obvious case is the bedroom, and South African summers make it a stronger case than most. Sunrise arrives before five in midsummer along much of the coast, and a standard translucent roller does little more than soften it. Shift workers who sleep in daylight, small children who wake with the sun, and media rooms where screen glare matters are the other three rooms where a blockout fabric is the correct specification rather than a preference.

The fit matters more than the fabric

A common disappointment is a genuine blockout fabric that still lets the room glow at the edges. That is not the fabric failing. A roller blind is a flat panel in front of a window, and light will travel around any gap it is given. An inside-reveal fit looks neat but leaves a few millimetres either side by necessity. Fitting the blind outside the reveal, wider and taller than the opening, closes most of that path. Where true darkness is the goal, side channels or a cassette that the fabric runs inside will get a room to cinema-dark. Deciding this before ordering matters, because it changes the measurements.

The heat and cold you do not see

The same coating that stops light also slows heat. On a west-facing window a lowered blockout blind takes a real edge off late-afternoon heat load, and in winter it adds a useful layer against single glazing at night. In a country where most housing stock is single glazed, that quiet thermal work is worth as much as the darkness.

Living with them

Blockout rollers are among the lowest-maintenance coverings made. The fabric presents a flat face that collects little dust, a monthly wipe with a dry cloth keeps it clean, and there are no slats to bend. Operation is a chain or a spring, and motorised options remove even that. The fabric coating is the working part, so avoid folding or creasing it and it will last for years.
