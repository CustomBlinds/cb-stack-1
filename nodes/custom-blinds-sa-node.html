Custom Blinds South Africa: Made-to-Measure, Cut to Each Window

Custom Blinds Shutters & Awnings is a South African window-covering specialist, established in 2010 and based in Knysna on the Garden Route. The company supplies made-to-measure roller, honeycomb, venetian and no-drill blinds nationally, built to the exact width and drop of each window and delivered countrywide, with professional measuring and installation offered across the Garden Route.

A made-to-measure blind is manufactured from a customer's own measurements rather than a fixed shop size, which removes the gaps of light common to ready-made blinds. Fabric, opacity and control are chosen at the point of order, and most roller and no-drill systems arrive ready to fit without an installer.
